# Quality Checklist: Websites & Landing Pages

Verify landing pages and marketing websites prior to launch:

- [ ] **Instant Value Proposition**: The hero section communicates what the product is, who it is for, and why it is better within 5 seconds of scanning.
- [ ] **Dual Call-to-Action**: Clear primary CTA (visually high weight) paired with an exploratory secondary CTA (documentation, live demo, or video walkthrough).
- [ ] **Authentic Proof & Evidence**: Testimonials, benchmark numbers, logos, and case studies are 100% genuine; no placeholder fake quotes or fabricated metrics.
- [ ] **Real Interface Imagery**: Screenshots and previews show actual running product interfaces, not generic 3D abstract shapes or stock photos.
- [ ] **Interactive Copyable Snippets**: Code snippets have a 1-click copy button with instant "Copied!" checkmark feedback.
- [ ] **Comparison Transparency**: Comparison matrices reflect actual technical differences honestly without misleading competitor bashings.
- [ ] **Objection-Busting FAQ**: FAQ accordion covers critical purchasing or adoption concerns (pricing, privacy, migration, compatibility).
- [ ] **SEO & OpenGraph Tags**: Page title, meta description, favicon, OpenGraph card image, and canonical URL are configured and verified.
- [ ] **Fast Core Web Vitals**: LCP under 2.0s, CLS under 0.05, zero render-blocking unnecessary external scripts.
- [ ] **Clear Global Footer**: Contains copyright, terms, privacy policy, status page, GitHub repository link, and release notes.
